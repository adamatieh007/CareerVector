Inheritance Model

flowchart LR P[Computer Hardware Engineers] --> A[FPGA Engineer] P --> B[ASIC Design Engineer] P --> C[Embedded Hardware Engineer] P --> D[Digital Hardware Engineer] META[Parent validated evidence] --> P META --> M1[Skills and tasks] META --> M2[NCES compatible majors] META --> M3[BLS wages and outlook]

Specific role titles are not treated as independent fabricated occupations. O*NET alternate titles inherit evidence from their validated parent occupation while preserving the title that users actually recognize.

Optional ESCO Boundary

ESCO roles are appended only when the official English CSV package exists under data/raw/esco/. They do not automatically receive U.S. salary or CIP metadata because CareerVector avoids inventing unsupported cross-taxonomy mappings.
