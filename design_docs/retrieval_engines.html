Default Weighting

flowchart TB R[58 percent Retrieval] --> S[Combined score] A[24 percent Academic prior] --> S T[13 percent Role-title specificity] --> S O[5 percent Outlook] --> S

When academic or outlook data is unavailable, that missing weight is redistributed over available signals rather than being treated as a bad match.

Role Diversity

O*NET may contain many aliases under the same parent occupation. After scoring, CareerVector caps repeated roles from one parent SOC so the Top-K list does not become ten near-duplicates.
