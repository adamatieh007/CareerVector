Purpose

Version 0.4 changes the unit of retrieval from roughly one thousand broad occupations to tens of thousands of specific career-role titles. Those roles inherit validated O*NET evidence and are enriched with NCES academic mappings and BLS labor-market data. Optional ESCO data can add additional role and skill concepts.

Key Design Shift

flowchart LR A[1,016 broad occupations] --> B[O*NET alternate titles] B --> C[58K+ specific role records] C --> D[NCES major compatibility] D --> E[BLS wage and outlook metadata] E --> F[Optional ESCO enrichment] F --> G[Higher-resolution retrieval]

The language model is still downstream of retrieval. Improving the knowledge base improves TF-IDF, embeddings, and RAG simultaneously.
